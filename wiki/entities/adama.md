# ADAMA

ADAMA is a Jewish youth community project for young Russian-speaking new
immigrants in Israel. Its social content combines practical integration,
Hebrew and Israeli culture, Jewish identity and meaning, humor, events, and
community belonging.

See the full [project brief](../sources/adama-project-brief.md).

ADAMA is operated alongside [CBA Young](cba-young.md), but the communities have
separate brands, social accounts, content queues, design sources, and publishing
credentials. See [multi-account brand isolation](../concepts/multi-account-brand-isolation.md).
