# CBA Young

CBA Young is a community and social account operated alongside ADAMA but with
its own brand identity, content queue, approval inbox, platform credentials,
and design sources. The two communities may share agent infrastructure, never
brand assets or account state.

The connected `CBA.young — sandbox` Figma file belongs to CBA Young only.
