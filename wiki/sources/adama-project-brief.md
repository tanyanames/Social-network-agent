# ADAMA social media agent — project brief

Source: operator prompt, 2026-07-25.

## Goal

Build an autonomous social-media management agent for ADAMA, a Jewish youth
community for young Russian-speaking new immigrants in Israel. The MVP must be
highly autonomous in research, planning, drafting, critique, and queue
preparation, while every publication remains human-approved.

## Required flow

1. Analyze the existing account and content.
2. Research trends.
3. Produce weekly and monthly content plans.
4. Draft Reels scripts.
5. Structure carousels.
6. Generate captions and calls to action.
7. Critique virality and brand fit.
8. Create design briefs.
9. Prepare a publishing queue.
10. Require human approval before publishing.
11. Later add Instagram, Facebook, and Telegram publishing.
12. Later add analytics and growth recommendations.

## Content and brand

Formats include Reels, carousels, memes, stories, event announcements, photo
reports, Shabbat and educational posts, landing-page copy, and ads.

The style is young, fresh, bold, emotionally minimal, and built around ADAMA's
green/lime palette, large typography, Jewish meaning, Israeli life, immigrant
memes, and community energy.

Core topics include Jewish youth, aliyah, Hebrew, integration, Israeli slang,
holidays, Shabbat, Israel, Jewish thought, the Lubavitcher Rebbe, community,
events, Shabbatons, trips, and city groups.

## Current phase constraint

Repository research only. Do not implement the product and do not create skills
until the base repository has been selected.
